## Tips for Getting the Most Out of Early Access

- **Play the tutorial fully.** Grid battlers hide a lot of rules in their first hour, and skipping it makes later losses feel random instead of earned.
- **Turn on any available bug reporting tools.** Most developers read them, and a good report often gets fixed within a patch or two.
- **Keep a backup of your saves** if the game allows it. Progression resets do happen during development.
- **Avoid chasing the meta early.** Balance shifts; a strategy that dominates this patch may be nerfed the next.
- **Revisit the game after big patches.** A single update can change the feel of an entire roster.
- **Follow patch notes rather than reviews.** Reviews describe a moment in time; patch notes describe a direction.

One more habit worth building: write down what confused you after each session. If a mechanic is unclear to you, it is probably unclear to a few hundred other players, and that feedback is exactly what early access is designed to surface.
