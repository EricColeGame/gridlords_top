## How to Tell an Official Trailer From a Fan Edit

Fan trailers are impressive, and that is exactly the problem. A skilled editor can make a 30-second concept piece look convincing, especially on a phone screen. Knowing the warning signs saves you from repeating something that was never true.

| Red flag | What it usually means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Uploaded by an unfamiliar channel | Re-upload or fan edit | Search for the same footage on a verified channel |
| No description, platforms, or links | Not a sanctioned release | Treat every claim as unverified |
| Music that cuts abruptly or is unrecognizable | Assembled from stock or other games | Compare audio against the publisher's version |
| Logos that look slightly off | Deliberate imitation | Check against the studio's official branding |
| Comments disabled | Avoiding corrections | Find a sourced discussion thread instead |
| Date stamp older than the announcement | Recycled footage | Verify the original upload date |

The fastest test is still the simplest one. If you cannot trace a Gridlords official trailer back to a verified studio, publisher, or storefront page, assume it is not official until proven otherwise.

## Community Reports vs. Confirmed Details

| Claim you will see | Typical source | Confidence level | How to verify |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Release date confirmed" | Community posts | Low unless shown on screen | Look for an explicit date in official text |
| "It is coming to every platform" | Comment sections | Low | Check each storefront individually |
| "The trailer shows X mechanic" | Frame-by-frame threads | Medium | Pause the footage and look for a HUD element |
| "A developer said so in an interview" | Podcast or stream clips | Medium-high | Find the full interview, not a clip |
| "Pre-orders are live" | Aggregator sites | Low | Confirm on the official store page |

A simple rule keeps you accurate: a claim is confirmed when you can point to a primary source — the trailer itself, a store page, or a direct quote from the studio. Everything else is player experience, and it deserves that label when you pass it along.
